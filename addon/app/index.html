A plain LAN address for this add-on, not the Home Assistant ingress link. Feeders fetch custom sounds directly over the network, they can't use your logged-in browser session. Required before you can push a custom sound to a feeder.

Sound Library

Shared across all feeders. Push any of these to a specific feeder from its Maintenance tab.

Any common audio format works (MP3, WAV, M4A, etc.), it's converted automatically. Recording needs HTTPS or localhost access to your browser's microphone, which most home setups reached over plain HTTP don't have, Upload File works everywhere and is the reliable option.

Downloads a rolling log of recent raw device MQTT traffic, including any device this app doesn't recognize (marked UNRECOGNIZED). WiFi name and any local audio server URL are redacted, but this file still includes your device serial numbers, so please email it to github@mega-city.com rather than posting it publicly when reporting an unsupported or misbehaving device.
